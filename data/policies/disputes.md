# Dispute policy

## How to raise a dispute
A customer can dispute a charge or a balance by phone or through the Horizon
Bank portal, within 30 days of the statement date. The customer should state
which amount they dispute and why.

## What happens during a dispute
Collection activity on the disputed amount is paused while the dispute is
open. Horizon Bank resolves disputes within 21 days and informs the customer
of the outcome by SMS. Amounts not under dispute remain payable as usual.
