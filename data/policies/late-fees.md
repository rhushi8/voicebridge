# Late fee policy

## When a late fee applies
A late fee applies if the outstanding balance is not paid by the due date.
There is a 3-day grace period after the due date during which no fee is
charged.

## How much is the late fee
The late fee is a flat 200 rupees per month after the grace period, charged
at most 3 times. Late fees are added to the outstanding balance and appear on
the next statement.

## Can a late fee be waived
A first-time late fee can be waived if the full balance is paid within 15
days of the original due date. Waiver requests are handled by a human agent,
not by the automated assistant.
