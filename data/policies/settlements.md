# Settlement policy

## Who qualifies for a settlement
Accounts more than 30 days past their due date may qualify for a one-time
lump-sum settlement at a reduced amount. The available discount depends on
how long the account has been overdue. Accounts that are current or less
than 30 days overdue do not qualify.

## How a settlement works
A settlement is agreed on the call, and the customer receives an SMS payment
link to complete it. The settled amount must be paid within 7 days of the
agreement, or the offer lapses and the full balance becomes due again. Once
the settlement payment clears, the account is closed and reported as settled.
