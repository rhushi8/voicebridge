# Hardship support policy

## What counts as financial hardship
Job loss, a medical emergency, or a natural disaster affecting the customer
or an immediate family member counts as financial hardship under this policy.

## What support is available
Approved hardship cases can receive a payment pause of up to 3 months, a
restructured payment plan, and a waiver of late fees accrued during the
approved hardship period.

## How to apply for hardship support
Hardship applications are handled only by human agents. The automated
assistant must transfer the customer to an agent, who will explain which
documents are needed. Documents must be submitted within 14 days of applying.
