# Payment plan policy

## Who is eligible for a payment plan
Customers with an outstanding balance of 5,000 rupees or more can request a
payment plan. Accounts under active dispute are not eligible until the dispute
is resolved.

## Payment plan tenures and interest
A payment plan can be taken over 6, 9, or 12 monthly installments. Interest is
charged at 12% per annum on the reducing balance, and the monthly installment
(EMI) is calculated using the standard reducing-balance formula. Longer tenures
mean smaller monthly installments but more total interest.

## How to set up a payment plan
A plan can be set up during a call with our assistant or through the Horizon
Bank portal. The customer receives an SMS confirmation within 24 hours, and
the first installment is due within 7 days of setup.

## Fees and missed installments
There is no setup fee for a payment plan. A missed installment cancels the
plan and the full remaining balance becomes due immediately.
