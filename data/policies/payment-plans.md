# Payment plan policy

## Who is eligible for a payment plan
Customers with an outstanding balance of 5,000 rupees or more can request a
payment plan. The balance can be split into up to 6 monthly installments, with
a minimum installment of 1,000 rupees. Accounts under active dispute are not
eligible until the dispute is resolved.

## How to set up a payment plan
A plan can be set up during a call with our assistant or through the Horizon
Bank portal. The customer receives an SMS confirmation within 24 hours, and
the first installment is due within 7 days of setup.

## Fees and interest on payment plans
There is no setup fee for a payment plan. No additional interest is charged
as long as installments are paid on time. A missed installment cancels the
plan and the full remaining balance becomes due.
